v0.5.0 — visible installation phases and resumable checkpoints

- Added a 12-phase checklist with an overall completion percentage.
- Every phase explains what is being changed and why before it starts.
- Substeps use visible status markers: active, completed, warning and failed.
- Completed phases are recorded in .install-state.json and shown after resume.
- Added the same progress model to single-node and three-node, online and
  air-gapped paths.
- Multi-node online now separates OS checks, verified kernel reboots, UFW,
  mounts, stanctl installation, cluster installation and health checks.
- Single-node kernel setup is again executed and verified rather than being
  silently assumed.
- Passwords and Instana keys remain excluded from state, config and log files.

Progress is informational and does not bypass resource validation. Resume still
verifies existing cloud resources before continuing.
